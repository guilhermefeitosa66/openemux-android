# Ready-made themes: artwork and colors

The five ready-made themes (issue 1018) and where each part comes from. The pictures and the
`theme.yaml` files the app ships are generated by `tools/gen-ready-themes.py`.

## Artwork

Every picture is **original**: drawn for OpenEmux by `tools/gen-ready-themes.py` from basic shapes
(circles, paths, rectangles), with no image copied, traced or derived from another project, wallpaper
site or theme (Caelestia included). License: **MIT**, like the app's Kotlin code.

| Theme | File | What it shows | Source | License |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pastel | `pastel/library.svg` | Clouds, sparkles and pastel hills | Original (OpenEmux) | MIT |
| Dracula | `dracula/library.svg` | A crescent moon, bats and pine hills at night | Original (OpenEmux) | MIT |
| Kitty | `kitty/library.svg` | A cat sitting by a ball of yarn, floating hearts | Original (OpenEmux) | MIT |
| Neon | `neon/library.svg` | A striped sun, mountains and a perspective grid | Original (OpenEmux) | MIT |
| Tokyo Night | `tokyo-night/library.svg` | A city skyline with lit windows and a moon | Original (OpenEmux) | MIT |

The app ships them as `src/main/assets/themes/<id>/images/library.webp` (720 x 1280). The desktop ships
its own copy, made from these SVGs.
